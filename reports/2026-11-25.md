## Findings

### High

**H1. A device that leaves and comes back: the website and one Dyne page say more than Dyne has shown.**
- Dyne `README.md` and `docs-site/status.md`: after power returns, the device reconnects in about 40 s
  "but does not yet rejoin the computation" (dyne #131).
- Website, "What runs today": "the run resumes when the device returns".
- Dyne `docs-site/concepts.md` line 85: "rejoined about 40 s later".
- Impact: a public claim of something not yet shown on hardware.

**H2. The website and the organization profile do not list erg, gauss and poise.**
- Website repository table and `.github/profile/README.md` list only dyne and barye; `repos.md` lists seven.

### Medium

**M1. IMU result on the website reads as fusion on the devices.**
- Website: "Seven accelerometers fused on one board — static noise about 2.2× lower".
- Dyne `docs-site/results/imu-2026-11-21.md`: the run recorded the sensors and analysed them on a PC; the
  on-device fusion solver was not running.

**M2. Wave-equation stability condition on the website omits its assumption.**
- Website: "wave scheme stable for Courant number ≤ 1/√2".
- Dyne `docs-site/theory.md`: proven for an infinite uniform grid; checked numerically on bounded grids
  (0.70765 with fixed-value walls; 1/√2 with zero-gradient and absorbing walls).

**M3. erg README status is out of date.**
- README: "just started. Nothing here runs yet."
- erg main has a first MJCF model with a 10 s standing test, design records (scope, robot and comparison,
  expected differences), the Dyne submodule with a bit-exact check, and CI.

### Low

**L1. No license in the website and `.github` repositories.** The four code repositories follow
`contracts.md`. The website and `.github` have none, so their text and code are "all rights reserved" by default.

**L2. Example MAC address in Dyne firmware comments.** `node-esp32/main/dyne_node_id.h` and
`dyne_node_id_core.h` use `24:0A:C4:12:34:56` as an example. It looks fictional (vendor prefix + `12:34:56`),
but `contracts.md` asks for pseudonyms of the form `02:00:00:…` in public repositories.

**L3. "Maximum error" without saying which error.** Website stability row: "never increase the maximum
error". Per `glossary.md`, say numerical error (split vs. reference) or estimation error (estimate vs.
ground truth).

### Checked and fine

- `LICENSE-MIT` copyright line identical in dyne, erg, gauss, poise; dyne `CITATION.cff` license field follows `contracts.md`.
- Recent commits are authored with the GitHub no-reply address; erg commits keep `Co-Authored-By`.
- The e-mail address in `.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` is the published contact chosen by the maintainer.
